# Community code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Welcome constructive questions and disagreement.
Evaluate ideas and evidence, and give specific, actionable feedback.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, and publishing private information are prohibited.
Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report concerns privately to a repository maintainer through an established contact channel. Reports
are handled with care for the privacy and safety of everyone involved.

This policy applies to project repositories, discussions, events, and other spaces maintained by the
project. Maintainers are responsible for fair and consistent enforcement.
