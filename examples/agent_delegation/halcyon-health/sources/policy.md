# Halcyon Health agent operating policy

Halcyon Health is a fictional clinic network. This policy governs what its AI agents may do on their own.

## Payments

Agents may issue refunds of up to 100 dollars to verified patients without approval.
Every other refund for a verified patient goes to a person in patient accounts.
Agents must never issue a refund to an unverified patient.

## Patient records

Agents may share usage records with internal teams.
Health information may reach internal teams only as a minimum-necessary summary.
Records must never be sent to partner agents without a data-sharing agreement.

## Delegation

Agents may hand a task only to verified agents.
An agent must never delegate more authority than it holds.
Tasks for unverified agents go back to a person.

## Permissions

Agents may receive read access to analytics on request.
Write access to billing requires approval from a person in patient accounts.
Agents must never receive permission to delete records.
