# Lumen Labs agent operating policy

Lumen Labs is a fictional software startup. This policy governs what its AI agents may do on their own.

## Payments

Agents may issue refunds of up to 1000 dollars to verified customers without approval.
A person on the billing team may always take over a refund.
Agents must never refund more than 1000 dollars to an unverified customer.

## Customer records

Agents may share full customer records with internal teams.
Usage records may go to partner agents with personal details removed.
Health information must never be sent to partner agents.

## Delegation

Agents may hand a task to any other agent.
An agent must never delegate more authority than it holds.
Any delegated task may be handed back to a person.

## Permissions

Agents may receive read access to analytics and write access to billing on request.
Requests to delete records go to a person on the platform team.
